Request hold. Each request carries a hold of $3,998 for flights within the United States or $7,998 for international flights, or an account balance of $10,000 or more in place of the hold. The hold is applied to your booking. If you do not book within 48 hours of the first quote, or no Operator quotes within 24 hours, the hold is released. Allow 3 business days.
Price. You pay the Operator's quoted price plus taxes and government fees. EXJET adds nothing.
Taxes. US domestic flights carry 7.5% federal excise tax and a per passenger segment fee.
Payment. Payment is due within 24 hours of signing the Operator's agreement. Pay by bank wire, account balance or card.
Wire and balance saving. Payment by wire or account balance receives up to 5% off, reflecting the card processing cost that is avoided.
Account balance. Tiers of $100,000, $150,000 and $250,000. Held in escrow. 100% refundable on request. No expiry.
Cancellation, changes, delays and refunds for a flight. These follow the Operator's charter agreement. EXJET has no separate cancellation schedule and cannot override the Operator's terms.
Chargebacks. You agree to raise any flight dispute with the Operator under its agreement before disputing a charge.
