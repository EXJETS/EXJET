Plans. Individual: $1,499 per month, one user. Business: $1,499 per month plus $150 per month for each additional user.
Billing. Billed in advance by term: quarterly ($4,497), 6 months ($8,994) or annual ($17,988). Each additional Business user: $450, $900 or $1,800 per term. There is no monthly billing.
Renewal. Membership renews automatically for the same term at the then current price until you cancel in Account. We send a reminder before each renewal.
Cancelling. You can cancel at any time in Account. Access continues to the end of the paid term. Fees for a term already started are not refunded, except where the law requires.
What membership includes. Access to send requests, receive Operator quotes at Operator prices with no markup, and book on the platform. Membership does not include any flight and does not guarantee that any aircraft is available.
Lapse. If payment fails, requests are paused until the membership is paid. Existing bookings are not affected.
