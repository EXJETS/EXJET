Eligibility. You hold a current FAA Part 135 certificate, or the equivalent from your authority, and list only aircraft on your operations specifications and on the EXJET approved fleet list.
No fees. EXJET charges Operators no listing fee and no commission.
Your price and your contract. You quote your own price. The client sees it unchanged. You supply your own charter agreement naming the client as charterer. EXJET is not a party.
Operational control. You hold operational control of every flight at all times and are solely responsible for airworthiness, crew, insurance, safety and regulatory compliance.
Insurance. You maintain liability insurance at limits customary for the aircraft and name the charterer as required by your agreement.
Indemnity. You defend and indemnify EXJET against claims arising from your aircraft, crew, flights or agreement.
Payout. Client funds are released to you as set out in your onboarding schedule.
Non circumvention. For 24 months you will not book clients introduced through EXJET outside the platform.
Accuracy. You keep your fleet, availability and contact details current and answer requests promptly.
