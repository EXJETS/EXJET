EXJET lists aircraft offered for sale by their owners and listing brokers. EXJET is a marketplace. EXJET is not the seller, is not an aircraft dealer or broker, does not hold title, and does not act as agent for the buyer or the seller unless a separate signed agreement says so.
Listing details (year, serial, hours, price, equipment, photos) come from sellers and third party sources. They may be wrong or out of date, and an aircraft may already be sold or withdrawn. EXJET does not inspect aircraft or verify listings.
Buyers must do their own diligence: title and lien search, logbook and records review, pre purchase inspection, and escrow through a licensed title company. Nothing on EXJET is an offer by EXJET to sell an aircraft.
Sellers confirm they own the aircraft or are authorized in writing to offer it, that the details are true, and that they hold the rights to every photo they supply.
When you send an inquiry, EXJET shares your name, email and phone with the seller or listing broker.

Listing requirements. Only an owner, or a broker or representative holding a signed mandate from the owner, may list. The seller must supply proof of ownership, the mandate where a broker lists, and a current maintenance status report, and must keep the published status (total time, engines, inspections due and not due) accurate. "Verified" means EXJET compared the listing with the documents supplied. It is not an inspection, an appraisal or a warranty of airworthiness or title.
