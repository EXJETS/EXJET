Every passenger must be listed on the manifest with full legal name, date of birth and weight, and must carry valid government photo ID, and a passport for international flights.
Every adult passenger accepts the release before the manifest is sent. A parent or guardian accepts for a minor.
The pilot in command has final authority over the flight, including refusing boarding and changing or cancelling the flight for safety.
No illegal substances, no undeclared hazardous materials, no weapons except as the Operator and the law allow. No smoking unless the Operator allows it.
Pets, special items and catering are subject to the Operator's approval and price.
Damage to the aircraft is charged by the Operator under its agreement.
