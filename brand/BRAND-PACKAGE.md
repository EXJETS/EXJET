# EXJET brand package

This document is the portable brand brief for EXJET. Paste it into a new design or content conversation to keep future work aligned with the website.

## Brand position

EXJET is a Dallas-based private aviation brokerage serving passenger charter, group travel, cargo, membership, and aircraft sales. The brand should feel like a precise private flight department with global reach, not a mass-market marketplace.

Primary line: **Global Access, On-Demand.**

Service promise: one accountable relationship from the first request through trip completion.

Public role language: EXJET arranges charter flights as a broker. Flights are operated by the licensed third-party air carrier identified for each trip. Avoid language that implies EXJET owns or operates the broad brokered fleet.

## Brand personality

- Quiet confidence
- Exact, direct, and discreet
- Technical without feeling cold
- Premium without decorative excess
- Human and accountable

## Visual system

### Logo

Use only the exact supplied EXJET wordmark and chevron. Never redraw, retype, stretch, outline, or reinterpret the mark. Preserve the spelling **EXJET**.

- Dark mark: `/public/brand/exjet-mark.svg`
- Light mark: `/public/brand/exjet-mark-light.svg`
- Minimum digital width: 104 px
- Clear space: at least the height of the chevron on every side
- Use the light mark on graphite or photography. Use the dark mark on paper or white.

### Color

| Name | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flight Graphite | `#0B0C0E` | Primary dark field, premium sections |
| Carbon | `#181A1D` | Secondary panels and depth |
| Hangar Paper | `#F4F4F2` | Primary light background |
| Pure White | `#FFFFFF` | Type on dark, space, highlights |
| System Gray | `#6D7075` | Supporting copy and metadata |
| Airframe Line | `rgba(11,12,14,.16)` | Rules and grid lines |

Do not add a saturated accent color unless it serves a real state such as safety, warning, or availability.

### Typography

Primary family: Helvetica Neue, Helvetica, Arial, or a metrically compatible grotesk.

- Display: regular weight, tight tracking, compact line height
- Body: regular weight, 1.65 to 1.8 line height
- Labels: 10 to 12 px equivalent, semibold, uppercase, 0.14 to 0.20 em tracking
- Italics are reserved for the second thought in a headline, not for body emphasis

### Layout

- Strong grid, visible rules, generous white space
- Editorial scale contrast between headline and metadata
- Photography should feel architectural, mechanically credible, and calm
- Mobile is the primary constraint. Important actions must appear before scrolling
- Avoid rounded cards, glassmorphism, stock luxury motifs, gold gradients, and decorative aviation clichés

## Voice and writing

Lead with the mission and the outcome. Keep sentences direct. Prefer precise verbs: source, evaluate, coordinate, confirm, arrange.

Use:

- “EXJET sources the strongest available options for the mission.”
- “One advisor stays with the trip from request through arrival.”
- “Wi-Fi is subject to aircraft and route availability.”

Avoid:

- “Our fleet” when referring to brokered aircraft
- Unsupported superlatives
- Ownership or operating-carrier implications
- Long strings of em dashes
- Generic phrases such as “luxury redefined”

## Asset production notes

Aircraft images should be delivered as 3840 x 2160 landscape and 1800 x 2400 portrait pairs where possible. Keep the entire aircraft inside a 10% safe margin. Supply clean images without baked-in type, watermarks, people, or inaccurate logos. Website image derivatives should be WebP or AVIF, with meaningful alt text and responsive crops.
